The kernel and the simulator produce identical counts for demand, FIFO, LRU and known-next-use on the 2x16 replay trace at 32 KiB, asserted with no tolerance by crates/mlos-cli/tests/replay.rs (a TCG boot test, ~2 min). The kernel gained Manager::policy and make_room in the fault path, the 'replay POLICY' verb, and 'mlos run --run SCRIPT'. The trace reaches the guest on the model disk after the weights (mlos_synth::disk::TRACE_AT), not a second virtio-blk. Four disagreements were found and fixed, each a real bug only the comparison could see: residency read from tier (KV starts Warm), cyclic streams (KV accumulates; 27 reads), the simulator's budget being a byte count rather than a first-fit arena (8 reads; mlos-sim now links mlos-arena, Arena has a lifetime, Occupancy::fits is shared), and policy ties broken by enumeration order (5 reads; both policies now tie-break on object id). The verdict's next-use numbers moved by at most a point and the baselines not at all; docs/status.md has the table and the account. mlos-objman and mlos-objtab test files had silently stopped compiling (not in default-members) and were fixed. sw-checklist: 10 warnings, five carried plus five documented one-item exceptions.
