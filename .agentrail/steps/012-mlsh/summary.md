PRD gate G1 closed: MLOS reaches a shell. New mlsh crate (line buffer, dispatcher, four verbs: help/mem/dev/ticks) plus mlos-queue, a standalone lock-free byte queue. Input is queued by the interrupt handler and drained by the idle loop, because running a command inside a handler holds the interrupt active -- silencing the console and stopping the timer. Echo moved from driver to shell, where backspace can know whether the line is empty. Tick printing removed in favour of a  verb borrowing the counter, since the console now belongs to the shell. Verified with vhs driving a real pty: help/dev/mem/ticks all answer correctly and an unknown command is reported. Retired 5 sw-checklist warnings, and the churn found three real seams: the byte queue is not shell-specific (now mlos-queue), reserving the loader's footprint belongs to Machine not its caller (Machine::reserving), and wfi is an architecture primitive not a boot detail (mlos_hal_aarch64::wait_for_interrupt). Two intermediate splits were worse than what they replaced and were undone. Updated docs/status.md: G1 done.
