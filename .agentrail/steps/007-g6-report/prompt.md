The G6 report. A session, a budget that shrinks while it runs, and the
ladder walked rung by rung: what was delivered against what was promised
(precision reached, worst period, peak resident), `Rc`, `Ph`, `Ks` and
`Ss` at each rung, on the real shape and the synthetic one, with the
commands that regenerate every number and what it does not show. In the
kernel too, matching the simulator to the integer on both architectures,
or the report says why not. If the system kills a session the ladder
could have degraded, say so and stop.
