FINDING 1: VZ does NOT need UEFI -- VZLinuxBootLoader takes the uncompressed raw arm64 image mlos build already produces, no firmware or disk; architecture.md claimed VZEFIBootLoader was required and was wrong, now corrected. FINDING 2: VZ provides no PL011 (its console is virtio), so MLOS boots, runs and says nothing -- the design working as intended. N2 therefore costs a virtio-console driver, inserted as 017-virtio-console (also the first piece of mlos-provider-virtio that M2 needs). FINDING 3: EDK2 rejects the raw Image with 'Image type X64 can't be loaded on AARCH64 UEFI system' -- it wants PE/COFF and misreads the Image header as a PE machine type; Linux solves this by making code0 = MZ with res5 holding the PE offset. Inserted as 016-efi-stub. Neither blocks anything while VZLinuxBootLoader works.
