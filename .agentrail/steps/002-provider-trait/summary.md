mlos-provider (trait, forbid unsafe) and mlos-provider-dram (resident tier, unsafe confined to it, bounds checking in its own module). Two design.md departures made and written back: resolve() removed because the object table already resolves an id to provider+handle, and a second lookup on the fault path is the worst place for one; and providers take a Located (id AND handle) because a block store needs the handle while a recompute provider needs the id to know which activation to rebuild. ObjectMeta gained an opaque handle. Cost is latency plus throughput rather than one number, since latency is paid once and throughput scales with size -- a 2 MB activation against a 40 MB expert comes out wrong from either alone; for_bytes is what eviction compares, which is why cost is on the trait at all. prefetch defaults to a no-op, honest for a medium that cannot get ready. 11 tests, four of them the DRAM bounds failure cases (below, above, straddling, overflowing).
